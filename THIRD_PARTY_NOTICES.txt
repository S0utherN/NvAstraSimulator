Circular field morphology references (0.2.0, local evaluation)
------------------------------------------------------------
DSS2 red photographic survey cutouts served through CDS hips2fits, using
CDS/P/DSS2/red. This research made use of the hips2fits service, developed
at CDS, Strasbourg, France. DSS material originates from the Digitized Sky
Survey / STScI and the contributing photographic surveys. Source overview:
https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/hips/CDS/DSS2/red/
https://alasky.cds.unistra.fr/hips-image-services/hips2fits
Cutouts are used for explicitly assumed morphology; not measured emission
line maps or calibrated radiance. The raw FITS are not redistributed.

Hipparcos and Tycho Catalogues, ESA 1997, ESA SP-1200; CDS catalogue I/239.
https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/ftp/I/239/ReadMe
HIP identifiers, ICRS positions at epoch J1991.25 and V magnitude rankings
are used in lightweight derived scene lists. Stellar spectral shape and
flux zero point are this project's assumptions, not ESA measurements.

The new wide fields reuse Finkbeiner (2003) Halpha v1.1 data distributed by
NASA LAMBDA, incorporating WHAM/VTSS/SHASSA; earlier acknowledgements apply.
Original masked datasets remain separately available in the simulator.
Sources are credited; full public redistribution permission review is
incomplete. This local delivery does not constitute a public publication.
